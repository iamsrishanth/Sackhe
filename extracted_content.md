## About Us Page (`/about`)

### 1. Title & Introduction
* **Header:** About Us
* **Description:** Leading the revolution in sustainable waste management through innovation and commitment to environmental protection.

### 2. Our Story
* **Tagline:** THE PATH TO ZERO WASTE.
* **Founded:** December 2023
* **Content:**
  * Sackhe Technologies was founded in December 2023.
  * Before building anything, the team researched real-time napkin disposal. They found that menstrual waste was being burned openly, buried carelessly, or mixed with household trash. No safe, affordable solution existed for sanitary napkin disposal. Communities, schools, and women had no good option.
  * Developed the **Smokeless Cycle Menstrual Waste Incinerator** – a clean, smoke-free prototype built with support from **KG Reddy College of Engineering & Technology** and tested at **KASE Innovation Cell**.
  * Funded under the **NIDHI-Prayas program** through **SRIX Innovation Cell**.
  * Developed a full-fledged **Solid Waste Management Product** for municipalities, industries, and large institutions.
  * First system deployed and operational at **Telangana Social Welfare Residential Girls school Narsingi**, inaugurated by Hon'ble MLA Shri T. Prakash Goud (Rajendranagar) in the presence of T. Krishna Mohan (Commissioner, Narsingi Municipality) and Dr. W. G. Prasanna Kumar (Senior Scientist, Telangana Pollution Control Board).

### 3. Mission & Vision
* **Our Mission:** To provide eco-friendly incineration solutions that protect community health and the environment.
* **Our Vision:** To become India's most trusted name in sustainable waste management.

### 4. Who We Serve (Our Reach)
* **Description:** Our solutions are designed to meet the diverse needs of various sectors, contributing to a more sustainable future for all.
* **Sectors (All Active):**
  * **Girls Schools & Colleges:** Providing safe, hygienic, and eco-friendly sanitary waste disposal solutions for students.
  * **Local Communities:** Serving grassroots communities with accessible and sustainable waste management solutions.
  * **Municipal Authorities:** Partnering with local governments to implement city-wide waste management systems.
  * **Environmental Ecosystems:** Contributing to environmental protection through innovative eco-friendly technologies.
  * **Public Health:** Promoting community health through safe and hygienic waste disposal methods.
  * **Future Generations:** Ensuring a sustainable and clean environment for generations to come.

### 5. Our Values
* **Innovation:** Pioneering sustainable solutions for complex environmental challenges.
* **Sustainability:** Committed to protecting our planet for future generations.
* **Excellence:** Delivering the highest quality products and services.
* **Integrity:** Building trust through transparent and ethical practices.

### 6. Our Journey (Timeline)
* **Dec 2023 - Company Founded:** Sackhe Technologies was founded with the tagline "THE PATH TO ZERO WASTE".
* **Dec 2023 - First Product:** Smokeless Incinerator prototype developed with support from KG Reddy College of Engineering & Technology and tested at KASE Innovation Cell.
* **Jan 2024 - Seed Support:** Received support from KGRCET to scale prototype development and technical validation.
* **Jun 2024 - NIDHI-Prayas Grant:** Secured funding under DST's NIDHI-Prayas program through Srix Innovation Cell.
* **Jan 2025 - Eco-Pad Product Line:** Launched development of 100% biodegradable sanitary pad made from Organic cotton.
* **Nov 2025 - Solid Waste Management System:** Built and began testing the comprehensive system for municipalities.
* **Nov 2025 - Deployment at Narsingi:** First system deployed at Telangana Social Welfare Residential Girls School, Narsingi.
* **Present - Scaling & Impact:** Deploying pilots across Hyderabad institutions, working towards national rollout and international eco-certification.
